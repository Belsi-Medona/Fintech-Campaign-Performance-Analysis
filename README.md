# Fintech Campaign Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes fintech campaign performance across different customer types, communication channels, and monthly periods using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on understanding campaign effectiveness through key business metrics such as logins, conversions, yield, spend, revenue, and ROI.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business runs customer acquisition and engagement campaigns across multiple channels and customer segments.

However, campaign performance can vary significantly based on:

- Customer type
- Communication channel
- Monthly campaign performance

The objective of this analysis is to identify these performance patterns and generate data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The project aims to:

- Analyze overall campaign performance
- Compare Fresh and Repeat customer performance
- Evaluate channel-level conversion and ROI
- Identify monthly performance trends
- Understand revenue and spend patterns
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 📊 KPI Framework

The analysis evaluates the campaign funnel through the following metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Delivery Rate | Delivered ÷ Sent |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ Delivered |
| Login Rate | Logins ÷ Clicks |
| Yield | Conversions ÷ Logins |
| Conversion Rate | Conversions ÷ Delivered |
| CPL | Spend ÷ Logins |
| CPC | Spend ÷ Clicks |

## 📈 Key Findings

### Customer Type Performance

Repeat customers generated **2,500 conversions** with a **6.96% yield**, compared with **2,362 conversions** and a **5.21% yield** from Fresh customers.

Repeat customers generated approximately **₹29.95 lakh in revenue**, compared with approximately **₹26.33 lakh** from Fresh customers.

### Channel Performance

**RCS** generated the highest number of conversions at **1,751**, contributing **36.01%** of total conversions.

**Email** followed with **1,581 conversions**, contributing **32.52%**.

SMS generated **611 conversions (12.57%)**, while WhatsApp generated **919 conversions (18.90%)**.

### Monthly Performance

Monthly conversions ranged from **521 to 726**.

- January recorded the highest conversions at **726**
- August recorded the highest login volume at **12,179**
- August generated approximately **₹8.23 lakh in revenue**
- May recorded the highest yield at **6.19%**
- April recorded the lowest yield at **5.73%**

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen Repeat Customer Engagement

Repeat customers demonstrated higher yield and conversion performance. Focused engagement strategies can be explored to maintain and improve repeat-customer contribution.

### 2. Optimize Channel Performance

RCS and Email together contributed **68.53% of total conversions**. Channel-level performance should be reviewed regularly to identify opportunities for improving contribution from other channels.

### 3. Investigate Monthly Performance Variations

Monthly yield varied between **5.73% and 6.19%**. Campaign mix, customer type, and channel performance should be analyzed during higher- and lower-yield months to identify improvement opportunities.
